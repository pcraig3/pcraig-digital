About PCRAIG DIGITAL

PCRAIG DIGITAL is Paul Craig, a forward-deployed engineer and technical strategist with more than a decade of experience with modern public-sector service delivery — aka, “digital transformation”.

I’ve worked for the Government Digital Service in the UK, the Canadian Digital Service in Canada, and I’ve consulted for various US public sector organizations: Colorado’s Behavioural Health Administration, Health and Human Services , and New York State’s Department of Labor.

I work directly with stakeholders and users, prototype quickly, write production software, and I know the difference between moving fast and rushing to hit arbitrary deadlines. I like simple systems, open standards, accessible interfaces, user needs, and getting useful software out into the world.
